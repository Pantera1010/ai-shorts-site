Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

AI Shorts Pipeline is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Files in the owner's own Google Drive: it reads files the owner places in the tool's folder, and writes finished videos and reports back to that folder.
  • It does not access any other person's data.

What it does with that data

  • Uses it only to produce the owner's own videos.
  • Does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.
  • Does not use Google user data for advertising.
  • Does not use Google user data to train AI models.

Storage and removal

Data stays in the owner's Google Drive. The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
